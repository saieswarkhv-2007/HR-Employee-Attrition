About the Platform

The HR Employee Attrition AI Platform leverages advanced machine learning classification algorithms to analyze workforce data and identify key drivers behind employee turnover.

HR managers and organizational leaders can assess individual employee attrition probabilities, evaluate departmental risk factors, and formulate targeted retention policies to reduce costly employee turnover.
